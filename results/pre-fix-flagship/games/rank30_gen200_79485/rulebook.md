# gen200_79485

**Players:** 2  
**Cards:** Standard 52-card deck  
**Hand Size:** 3 cards per player  

## How to Play

This is a **shedding game** — the goal is to be the first player to empty your hand.

### Setup

Deal 3 cards to each player. Flip the top card of the deck to start the discard pile.

### On Your Turn

Play a card from your hand that **matches the suit or rank of** the top card of the discard pile.

If you cannot play, **draw 3 card(s)** from the deck.

### Winning

The first player to play all their cards wins. If the deck runs out, the player with the fewest cards wins.

## Special Cards

- **8s:** Can be played on any card
- **7s:** Can be played on any card
- **2s:** Can be played on any card
- **Js:** Can be played on any card
- **10s:** Can be played on any card
- **10s:** Can be played on any card

## Additional Rules

- One suit is designated as trump and beats other suits
- Earn bonus points for forming sets or runs
