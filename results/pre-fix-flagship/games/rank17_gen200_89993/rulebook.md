# gen200_89993

**Players:** 3  
**Cards:** Standard 52-card deck  
**Hand Size:** 10 cards per player  

## How to Play

This is a **trick-taking game** — players play one card each per trick.

### Setup

Deal 10 cards to each player.

### Playing Tricks

The lead player plays any card. Other players follow in order.

You **must follow the led suit** if you can. If you cannot, play any card.

**Lead restriction:** Cannot lead trump until trump has been played off-suit

The highest card of the led suit wins the trick, unless trumped. The trick winner leads the next trick.

### Scoring

Tricks are scored by the **point values** of the cards they contain (see scoring table). Highest score wins.

## Special Cards

- **8s:** Skip the next player's turn
- **Qs:** Skip the next player's turn
- **7s:** Can be played on any card
- **2s:** Reverse play direction

## Additional Rules

- Draw extra cards as a penalty in certain situations
