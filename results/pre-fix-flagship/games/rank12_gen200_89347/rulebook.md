# gen200_89347

**Players:** 2  
**Cards:** Standard 52-card deck  
**Hand Size:** 3 cards per player  

## How to Play

This is a **rummy game** — form melds to reduce your deadwood.

### Setup

Deal 3 cards to each player. Place one card face-up to start the discard pile.

### On Your Turn

1. **Draw** the top card from the discard pile
2. **Meld** (optional): groups of 4+ cards of the same rank. You may also extend existing melds on the table
3. **Discard** one card to the discard pile

### Knocking & Gin

You may **knock** when your deadwood is 27 points or less.

### Deadwood Values

- Face cards (J, Q, K): 10 points
- Ace: 1 point
- Number cards: face value

### Winning

The player with the lowest deadwood when someone knocks or goes gin wins the round.

## Special Cards

- **7s:** Can be played on any card
- **7s:** Next player draws 2 cards

## Additional Rules

- Score bonus points based on trick-like card combinations
