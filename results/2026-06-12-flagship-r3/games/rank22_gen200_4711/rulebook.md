# gen200_4711

**Players:** 2  
**Cards:** Standard 52-card deck  
**Hand Size:** 9 cards per player  

## How to Play

This is a **rummy game** — form melds to reduce your deadwood.

### Setup

Deal 9 cards to each player. Place one card face-up to start the discard pile.

### On Your Turn

1. **Draw** one card from the deck OR the top card from the discard pile
2. **Meld** (optional): groups of 3+ same-rank cards OR sequences of 3+ consecutive same-suit cards
3. **Discard** one card to the discard pile

### Knocking & Gin

You may **knock** when your deadwood is 28 points or less.

### Deadwood Values

- Face cards (J, Q, K): 10 points
- Ace: 1 point
- Number cards: face value

### Winning

The player with the lowest deadwood when someone knocks or goes gin wins the round.

## Additional Rules

- Draw extra cards as a penalty in certain situations
- Certain cards carry penalty points — avoid collecting them
