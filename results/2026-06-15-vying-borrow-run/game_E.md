# Card Game (rules below)

**Players:** 4  
**Cards:** Standard 52-card deck  
**Hand Size:** 5 cards per player  

## How to Play

This is a **vying / betting game** (poker family) — wager chips on hidden hands; the best poker hand at showdown takes the pot.

### Setup

Each player starts with 1000 chips. Every deal, each player is dealt 5 cards face-down.

### Each Deal

One player (rotating each deal) posts a big blind of 10 chips, so the first to act always faces a bet. Going around, each player in turn either:

- **Fold:** drop out, forfeiting any chips already in the pot, or
- **Call:** match the current bet, or
- **Raise:** match the bet and increase it by 10 (at most 3 raises per deal).

When nothing is owed you may **check** (stay in for free) instead of calling.

### Showdown

Once the betting is settled, the players still in reveal their hands and the best poker hand — pair, two pair, three of a kind, straight, flush, full house, four of a kind, straight flush — takes the pot. If everyone but one player has folded, that player takes the pot uncontested.

### Winning

Chips carry over across 12 deals. The player with the most chips at the end wins.
