# Card Game (rules below)

**Players:** 2  
**Cards:** Standard 52-card deck  
**Hand Size:** 12 cards per player  

## How to Play

This is a **fishing / capture game** (Casino / Scopa family) — you capture cards from a shared table into your own pile.

### Setup

Deal 12 cards to each player and lay 4 card(s) face-up on the table. The rest forms the stock.

### On Your Turn

Play **one card** from your hand and either:

- **Capture:** take every table card of the **same rank** as the card you played, or take any set of number cards whose values **sum** to your card's value — moving them and the card you played into your captured pile, or
- **Trail:** if you do not capture, leave the card you played face-up on the table.

Trailing is always legal, so you always have a move.

For summing, **Ace = 1** and number cards are worth their pips; face cards (J/Q/K) have no value and capture only by matching rank.

### Refilling and the Final Sweep

When every hand is empty, deal fresh hands from the stock. Once the stock can no longer deal a full round, the player who captured most recently takes any cards still on the table, and the game ends.

### Winning

Your score is the number of cards you captured plus the bonuses in the Additional Rules below (penalty cards count against you). The highest score wins.

## Additional Rules

- **Penalty cards:** at the end of each round, lose points equal to the value of any scoring cards (see Card Point Values) still in your hand or among the cards you captured — avoid collecting them
- **Meld bonus:** at the end of each round, score points for card combinations across your hand and the cards you have captured — a set of 3 or more of the same rank scores 5 points per card (a plain pair scores 2 per card), and a run of 3 or more consecutive cards in one suit scores 3 points per card (a 2-card run scores 1 per card)

## Card Point Values

| Card | Points |
|------|--------|
| Any of any suit | 1 |
