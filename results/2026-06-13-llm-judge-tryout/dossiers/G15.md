# G15

**Players:** 2  
**Cards:** Standard 52-card deck  
**Hand Size:** 13 cards per player  

## How to Play

This is a **shedding game** — the goal is to be the first player to empty your hand.

### Setup

Deal 13 cards to each player. Flip the top card of the deck to start the discard pile.

### On Your Turn

Play a card from your hand that **matches the suit or rank of** the top card of the discard pile.

If you cannot play, **draw 3 card(s)** from the deck.

### Winning

The first player to play all their cards wins. If no player can play and the deck runs out, the game ends in a draw.

## Special Cards

- **any 2:** Next player draws 2 cards
- **any 10:** Reverse play direction
- **any Diamond:** Can be played on any card
- **any Heart:** Can be played on any card
- **any Spade:** Can be played on any card
- **any Club:** Next player draws 2 cards

## Termination

This section reports whether the game can END -- separating how FAST the automated players reach a win from whether a win is reachable BY THE RULES. A low completion rate alone does NOT mean the game is broken: it may just be slow.

- Completion at the standard turn cap (260 turns): **100%** of 150 sampled games ended with a winner.
- Completion at a 4x extended turn cap (1040 turns): **100%**. Completion is roughly flat at the extended cap.

**Win-condition reachable (by design):**

- A player emptied their hand (the win condition) in completed sampled games, at a median of **turn 35**. The terminal state IS reachable by the rules.

- Has bidding/contract scoring: **no**
