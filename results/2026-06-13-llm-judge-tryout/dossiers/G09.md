# G09

**Players:** 2  
**Cards:** Standard 52-card deck  
**Hand Size:** 11 cards per player  

## How to Play

This is a **trick-taking game** — players play one card each per trick.

### Setup

Deal 11 cards to each player.

**Trump:** Spades are always trump

### Playing Tricks

The lead player plays any card. Other players follow in order.

You **must follow the led suit** if you can. If you cannot, play any card.

**Lead restriction:** Cannot lead trump until trump has been played off-suit

The highest card of the led suit wins the trick, unless trumped. The trick winner leads the next trick.

### Scoring

Each trick won is worth **1 point**. Highest score wins.

## Termination

This section reports whether the game can END -- separating how FAST the automated players reach a win from whether a win is reachable BY THE RULES. A low completion rate alone does NOT mean the game is broken: it may just be slow.

- Completion at the standard turn cap (110 turns): **100%** of 150 sampled games ended with a winner.
- Completion at a 4x extended turn cap (440 turns): **100%**. Completion is roughly flat at the extended cap.

**Win-condition reachable (by design):**

- Rounds reached completion in sampled games (all tricks were played out and scored). The terminal state IS reachable by the rules.

- Has bidding/contract scoring: **yes**
