# G14

**Players:** 3  
**Cards:** Standard 52-card deck  
**Hand Size:** 7 cards per player  

## How to Play

This is a **rummy game** — form melds to reduce your deadwood.

### Setup

Deal 7 cards to each player. Place one card face-up to start the discard pile.

### On Your Turn

1. **Draw** one card from the deck OR the top card from the discard pile
2. **Meld** (optional): groups of 3+ same-rank cards OR sequences of 3+ consecutive same-suit cards
3. **Discard** one card to the discard pile

### Going Out

You may **declare out** when your deadwood is 15 points or less.

### Deadwood Values

- Face cards (J, Q, K): 10 points
- Ace: 1 point
- Number cards: face value

### Winning

The player with the lowest deadwood when someone declares out or reaches zero deadwood wins the round.

## Termination

This section reports whether the game can END -- separating how FAST the automated players reach a win from whether a win is reachable BY THE RULES. A low completion rate alone does NOT mean the game is broken: it may just be slow.

- Completion at the standard turn cap (208 turns): **18%** of 150 sampled games ended with a winner.
- Completion at a 4x extended turn cap (832 turns): **7%**. Completion is roughly flat at the extended cap.

**Win-condition reachable (by design):**

- A going-out move became LEGAL in sampled games (a player's leftover-card total dropped to the threshold), first becoming legal at a median of **turn 9**. The terminal state IS reachable by the rules.
- At least one sampled game actually ended by going out (a player declared out or reached zero leftover cards).

- Has bidding/contract scoring: **no**
